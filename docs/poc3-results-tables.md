## 7. Limitations

From plan R9 section 12 (copied; `docs/poc3-implementation-plan-r9.md` line 325):

- The promise is an average over signs, not per class or per weather condition.
- Signs are assumed independent of each other. Signs from the same road or the same day may not be.
- BelgiumTSC views are simultaneous, so it cannot show approach-time earliness.
- All GTSRB results that use the POC-3 layer are exploratory, because every GTSRB track had been used before.
- alpha = 0.5% is not certifiable at these sample sizes.
- The escalation fallback (what the vehicle does next) is out of scope.
- The parts of the method exist separately in prior work. The contribution is the combination and the audit.

Added in POC-3 (recorded in the Part B entries; numbers read from the summaries):

- alpha 0.5% has no exact-binomial start at these Cert sizes: the exact binomial certified 0.5% in 0 of 25 (score, re-split) cells on GTSRB (Cert n = 784) and 0 of 25 on BelgiumTSC (Cert 464-472 units); in the BelgiumTSC resampling no draw had a start for any score. The cause is the power-aware start rule, not the test: the zero-error minimum for 0.5% is n_c = 460 (Stage 2 V1). (Source: `LOG_r9_stage3_entry.md` line 196; `LOG.md` line 1472.)
- S2 has low resolution on BelgiumTSC: with (a, b) = (0, 0.1) and 226 ICP calibration scores, its design grid has 3 points (S2-sum) and 3 (S2-min), against 30 for S1. Its pool error at view 1 is 3.35% against 1.44% for S1. Reported as a design-phase finding; S2 was not amended. (Source: `LOG.md` line 1490; `LOG_r9_stage5_entry.md` line 292.)
- The primary-alpha BelgiumTSC table is flat: at 3% S1, S3 and S4 certify grid point 0 (commit every unit at view 1) in 5, 5 and 5 of 5 re-splits, because the pool error at view 1 (1.44%) is below the budget. The primary alpha is not changed; the 1% table is reported beside it as pre-registered, not scored. (Source: `LOG_r9_stage6_entry.md` line 303.)
- DITS was not available (F-A3 FAIL: the download page could not be reached), so Stage 7 was dropped and earliness evidence is GTSRB only, labelled exploratory. (Source: `LOG_r9_stage1_entry.md` line 180; `runs/btsc/stage1/stage1_summary.json` line 690.)
